Conducting Performer · phrase libraries

Library Manager

Open the libraries from the Performer's card (LIBRARY/BASS.LIB, MELODY.LIB, DRUMS.LIB, PROGS.LIB) to preview, sort and tidy them. Open a second library in the right-hand pane to copy between the two. Nothing is written until you save.

Kits Patterns Library Controller
Keys and tips

Select: click a row; SHIFT + click a range; ⌘ (Ctrl) + click to add or remove one; click a style's name for all of it; ⌘A for everything (in the pane you clicked last).

Edit: ⌘C copy · ⌘X cut · ⌘V paste after the last selected row (into that row's style; with nothing selected, at the end) · ⌘D duplicate · ⌫ delete · ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move · ↑ ↓ (SHIFT) the selection · ⌘Z undo · SHIFT ⌘Z redo · SPACE play / stop.

Between libraries: bass lines and melodies paste into each other's library; drum patterns and progressions only into their own kind.

On the Performer: the library is browsed style by style, so saving puts each style's phrases together (where the style first appears); up to 16 styles. Pattern and drum chain entries remember the number of the phrase they were loaded from (LIB 012), and SAVE on S1 writes an edited pattern back to that number: after phrases are moved or deleted, load an entry from the library again before saving it back.
